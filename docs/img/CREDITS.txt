Licensing
---------
Neither image originated with Amryn's own photography or commissions, and
the licence they arrive under is not recorded here because it was not given.
Before this site is put in front of customers under a paid domain, confirm
that each one carries a licence permitting commercial use — the two usual
answers are a stock licence in Amryn's name or a written release from
whoever produced the image.

The typefaces in fonts/ have their provenance in fonts/LICENSE.txt, and the
brand artwork in brand/ is Amryn's own. These two files are the only assets
on the site whose licensing is unverified.

Rules

  1. Clear space around any lockup equals the cap height of the AMRYN letterforms.
  2. Minimum reproduction width: lockups 90px / 32mm, icon mark 24px / 8mm.
  3. Never recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to any mark.
  4. On backgrounds darker than 50% luminance use the white mark.
  5. Product wordmarks are set solid by design — do not respace them.
  6. Brand names are never uppercased: the solid capitalisation is part of the mark.
  7. Always carry ™ on Amryn and ® on AIGrowthIntelligence, DigitalTwin and OpportunityRadar.

AMRYN(TM) AIGrowthIntelligence(R) - BRAND ASSET PACK v2.0
Generated from founder-supplied source PNGs. Backgrounds keyed to transparency,
trimmed to the mark, colours un-multiplied so edges stay clean at any size.

FILE NAMING
  <name>.png      standard resolution
  <name>@2x.png   double resolution (use for print / large format)
  <name>-ondark   reversed for dark navy backgrounds (#081B33)
                  black elements -> white, brand blue lifted to #3E7BD6
                  for contrast on navy

USAGE RULES
  1. Clear space around any lockup = the height of the AMRYN cap height.
  2. Minimum reproduction width: lockups 90px / 32mm; icon mark 24px / 8mm.
  3. Never re-colour, stretch, rotate, outline or add effects to the marks.
  4. On backgrounds darker than 50% luminance use the -ondark variants.
  5. Product marks are set solid (no space around TM) by design - do not respace.
  6. Always carry (TM) on Amryn and (R) on AIGrowthIntelligence, DigitalTwin
     and OpportunityRadar.
  7. Where a product name is a title, use its wordmark file. In running prose
     it stays as text - a picture cannot wrap, be selected or be translated.

THE PACK, TO LOOK AT
  docs/brand-pack.html shows every asset above on light and on navy, with the
  palette, the typefaces and these rules. It carries the artwork and both faces
  inside it, so it survives being emailed or opened offline. Rebuild it with
  `node scripts/build-brand-pack.mjs` after changing anything in this folder.

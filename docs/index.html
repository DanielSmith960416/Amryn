                How it works

                Two views of the same business, one decision surface.

                Amryn™DigitalTwin®

                A continuously updated representation of the business, built from connected internal data — finance, sales, operations, people. It explains what is happening and why.

                Amryn™OpportunityRadar®

                Continuous external intelligence: tenders, demand shifts, competitor moves, pricing signals and market openings — scored and prioritised against your own capacity.

                Executive Command Centre

                Both views converge into one interface built for decision-makers: what matters, why it matters, what to do next, and what happened when you did it.

                Who it is for

                Any business building a growth engine.

                Amryn is not sized to a company, it is sized to a decision. Wherever management complexity is high and data is accessible — a single high-performing site, a multi-branch group, or a national operation — the same intelligence loop applies.

                Owner-led business

                One decision-maker holding everything. Amryn gives back the reporting hours and flags what would otherwise be missed.

                Growing company

                Enterprise operation
